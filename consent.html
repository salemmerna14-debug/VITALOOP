Bio-Telemetry Data Consent Protocol

Mission Artemis III Medical Operations Protocol

VITAIOOP collects physiological metrics, environmental conditions, and self-reported wellbeing indicators. Video frames for rPPG are processed strictly in browser memory and NEVER stored or transmitted.

Zero Face Video Transmission

Webcam video frames for rPPG optical scanning are processed strictly in browser memory. No face images or videos are ever uploaded or saved.

Encrypted Telemetry Channel

Bio-readings are encrypted at rest and in transit. Access is strictly controlled via Role-Based Access Control.

Mental Health Confidentiality

Self-reported psychological logs remain confidential to the astronaut unless explicit sharing with the Flight Surgeon is enabled.
